Overview

We are the ISC-DIP Turbo Electric Car Team - a passionate group of students driven by curiosity, innovation, and sustainability. We aim to design and build the most efficient electric kart that pushes the limits of creativity and engineering.

Our Mission

When we were chosen to represent ISC-DIP at the prestigious UAE National Electric Vehicle Grand Prix (EVGP), an event that challenges students across the globe to engineer, innovate, and compete with fully electric karts, it became more than a competition, it became a mission. We aim to embody the spirit of creativity, collaboration, and sustainability, driving toward victory and representing the passion and dedication of the Turbo Electric Car Team.
